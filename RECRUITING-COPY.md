## Merchant card (A6 or business-card size)

**Front**

> **Tired of paying for leads that go nowhere?**
> Backed. is a new way for tradespeople in Runcorn, Widnes and Warrington to get work.

**Back**

> - **£14.99 a month. That's it.** No pay-per-lead, no commission on your jobs.
> - **Fair queue.** Jobs are offered to members in turn, so the work doesn't all go to the biggest firm.
> - **Real local jobs**, from customers who've asked for a vetted tradesperson.
> - **Founding members keep £14.99 for as long as they stay.** The price goes up later for everyone else.
>
> Places are limited in each trade and town.
> **Scan to join the founding list** → [QR code]
> backedtrades.co.uk · Made by tradespeople, for tradespeople

## Facebook group post

Read each group's rules first. Many ban promotion. If so, message the admins and ask before posting.

**Version 1: short and direct**

> Hi all, I'm [name], one of two lads in [Runcorn/Widnes/Warrington] launching **Backed.**, a new site and app for getting local work.
>
> How it works: customers post a job and we offer it to one vetted local tradesperson, picked from a fair queue. It's a flat **£14.99 a month**. No pay-per-lead and no commission on what you earn.
>
> We're not live yet. We're building a founding list of local trades first, and founding members keep the £14.99 price for good. We only take a limited number per trade in each town.
>
> If you're interested, you can put your name down here: [link]
>
> Happy to answer any questions in the comments.

**Version 2: more conversational, for groups that dislike adverts**

> Question for the trades here. What do you currently pay to get work, and is it worth it?
>
> We're two local lads building something to fix what we hear about most: paying for leads that three other firms get too. Backed. is a flat £14.99 a month, no commission, and each job goes to one trade at a time in a fair queue.
>
> Not live yet. Founding members get the price locked. I'd value your honest opinions, and if it sounds useful you can join the list: [link]

## To check before using

- "Real local jobs" promises future volume. "Customers ask for a vetted tradesperson" is safer until customers are lined up.
- "One trade at a time" simplifies the rule (the platform picks one of three trades per job).
- "Vetted" isn't in the card headline; add it if trust should be a bigger part of the pitch.
- Put a name and phone number on the card: merchants and trades trust a person they can call.
