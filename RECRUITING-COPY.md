## App store listings (Play Console and App Store Connect)

These live in your accounts, so paste the lines below in. **Keep the price and any mention of paying out of the iOS
listing and its screenshots**: Apple only lets us sell the membership outside the app if the app never points to it
(App Review guideline 3.1.3(f)), the same reason the phone app never shows the price.

- **Short description (Play, 80 characters):** Local jobs for trades in Runcorn, Widnes, Warrington, Northwich and more.
- **Subtitle (Apple, 30 characters):** Local jobs for tradespeople
- **First line of the long description:** Backed. sends real local jobs to vetted tradespeople in Runcorn, Widnes,
  Warrington, Northwich, Winsford and Liverpool, through a fair queue, so every trade gets a turn.
- **Keywords (Apple):** add northwich, winsford, liverpool, runcorn, widnes, warrington
- **Also check:** "three towns" or the old town list anywhere else in the description or the reviewer notes.

## Facebook and Instagram ads

Set the ad's website URL to the tagged link, for example `backedtrades.co.uk/welcome?from=ad-plumbers-liverpool`,
so each ad is counted separately. Trades go to `/welcome` (the trade page), customers to `/`. The old coming-soon
sign-up page disappears once the full site is live, so change the destination on every running ad.

- **Trades, primary text:** Fed up with paying for leads that go nowhere? Backed. is a flat £14.99 a month in
  Runcorn, Widnes, Warrington, Northwich, Winsford and Liverpool. No pay-per-lead, no commission. Join before
  1 January 2027: pay £14.99 once and you're covered until then.
- **Trades, headline:** £14.99 a month. No pay-per-lead.
- **Customers, primary text:** Need a job doing? Post it once and Backed. matches you with one vetted, insured local
  tradesperson in Runcorn, Widnes, Warrington, Northwich, Winsford or Liverpool. Free for customers.
- **Customers, headline:** One vetted local trade. No ring-round.
- **Targeting:** add Northwich, Winsford and Liverpool to the location settings, with a separate ad set per town so
  you can compare them (and so you don't spend on a town with no trades yet).

## To check before using

- "Real local jobs" promises future volume. "Customers ask for a vetted tradesperson" is safer until customers are lined up.
- "One trade at a time" simplifies the rule (the platform picks one of three trades per job).
- "Vetted" isn't in the card headline; add it if trust should be a bigger part of the pitch.
- Put a name and phone number on the card: merchants and trades trust a person they can call.
